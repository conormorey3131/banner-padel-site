What is Padel Tennis?

Padel Tennis, commonly known as Padel, is one of the world's fastest-growing sports — a fun, fast-paced mix of tennis and squash that's easy to learn and addictive to play.

It's played in doubles on an enclosed court, slightly smaller than a tennis court, with walls that you can play the ball off (like squash). You use solid rackets and soft tennis-style balls, and points are scored just like in tennis.

Why People Love It

  • Easy to pick up – perfect for beginners
  • Fast and exciting rallies – always something happening
  • Social and inclusive – ideal for groups, friends, and clubs
  • Great for fitness – without being too intense or intimidating

Who Can Play?

Padel is for everyone – from kids and total beginners to seasoned racket sports players. It's low-impact and played at your own pace, so whether you're here for a laugh, a workout, or some serious competition, you'll feel right at home.
