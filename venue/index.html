The venue

Built to be worth staying in.

Six Super Panoramic indoor courts under one roof in Ennis, with a café and lounge that make it easy to turn a 90 minute booking into an evening.

Facilities

Everything the session needs.

Nothing here is an afterthought — the courts, the lighting and the space around them were all specified together.

Six Super Panoramic courts

Six Super Panoramic indoor courts featuring Mondo XN Supercourt turf, designed to deliver a premium playing experience all year round.

Café & lounge

Proper coffee, cold drinks and a comfortable place to sit, catch up and watch the action on court.

Changing facilities

Changing rooms and showers on site, so you can come straight from work and go straight on somewhere afterwards.

Racket hire & balls

Rackets are available to hire and balls are available to buy at reception.

Parking on site

Off-street parking right at the door, which matters when there are four of you arriving separately for a 7pm slot.

Events & private hire

Leagues, Americanos, tournaments, corporate days and private events — we can take over multiple courts at once.

First visit

What to expect.

Arrive ten minutes early

Enough time to check in at the desk, pick up hire rackets and get a look at the court before your slot starts.

Wear clean trainers

Clean sports or court shoes with good grip are recommended. Please make sure footwear is clean before entering the courts.

Bring a water bottle

There is water on site and the café is open, but a bottle courtside saves you the walk between games.

No kit needed

Rackets are available to hire and balls are available to buy at reception. Turn up with trainers and you are ready to play.
