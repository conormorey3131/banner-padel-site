The pathway

Wherever you're starting from.

01

Never played

Start on a beginner group clinic. You will serve, rally and use the back wall inside the first session.

02

Played a few times

Move into the improver groups, where the focus shifts to positioning and knowing when to lob.

03

Playing weekly

One-to-ones to sharpen individual shots, plus club leagues to test them under pressure.

04

Competing

Tournament prep, pairs work and tactical sessions with a coach who has watched you play.

Corporate & groups

Bring the whole team.

Team days, birthdays, stags, hens, club nights and work socials. We can put coaches on multiple courts at once, run an Americano format so nobody sits out, and have the café ready afterwards. It works just as well for a group who have never held a racket as for one that plays weekly.
