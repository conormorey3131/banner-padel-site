MORE THAN A GAME.

Padel is the easiest racket sport to fall in love with. Four players, glass walls, one rally that goes on far longer than it should — and everyone, whatever their level, in the same game.

We built Banner Padel so the people of Clare wouldn’t have to travel to play. Super Panoramic glass courts, proper lighting, no weather to work around, and a clubhouse worth staying in afterwards.

Never played?

You'll be rallying inside ten minutes.

Padel is doubles-only, played on an enclosed court about a third smaller than a tennis court. The walls are in play, like squash. The rackets are solid and light, the serve is underarm, and the scoring is the same as tennis. It is genuinely one of the easiest sports there is to pick up on day one.
